Competition announcement Session 5 / 2025

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, headquartered in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, no. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition, within the project PN-IV-P7-7.1-PED-2024-2026, contract 137PED/2025, for 2 research positions, as follows:

  • research assistant in medicine, PhD student, COR 221110
    • duration of the individual employment contract (CIM): 9 months, until 30.06.2026
    • minimum gross salary for the position: 1.600 RON
    • part-time work: ¼ of full time (2 hours / day, 10 hours / week)
  • technician with higher education (equivalent to research assistant) COR 226305 (equivalent)
    • duration of the individual employment contract (CIM): 15 months, until 31.12.2026
    • minimum gross salary for the position: 1.900 RON
    • part-time work: ¼ of full time (2 hours / day, 10 hours / week)
  • the application file in electronic format can be sent to daniela.lungu@ivb.ro
  • the competition will take place at the headquarters of the National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”;
  • the schedule of the competition tests, the topics and the bibliography are posted in the section dedicated to the competition on the website ivb.ro;
  • the complete list of documents in the application file is in accordance with art 5 of the Framework Methodology for competitions to fill vacant research positions, posted in the section dedicated to the competition on the website ivb.ro;
  • the job description, the application form, the declaration of consent for the processing of personal data, the declaration of agreement to the publication of the competition results on the institute’s website, the declaration on one’s own responsibility that the information in the application file is true and refers to one’s own activities and achievements, and the INCDVB-specific evaluation form are posted in the section dedicated to the competition on the website ivb.ro;
  • works that are not available in electronic format will be submitted as copies

Application files can be submitted daily between 05.08.2025 – 03.09.2025, from 13:00 – 15:00, at the Human Resources Office. Further information at www.ivb.ro.
