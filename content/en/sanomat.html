SANOMAT New diagnostic and treatment methodologies: current challenges and technological solutions based on nanomaterials and biomaterials

Component project 1

Development of bioactive nanostructures integrated into therapeutic and diagnostic systems

General objective: Development of technologies for obtaining, analysing, testing and optimising nanostructured systems with properties suitable for use in antitumour and anti-infective therapeutic systems, respectively.

Specific objectives:

  • Synthesis of Fe3O4, MnFe2O4 and Mn1-xZnxFe2O4 nanoparticles with controlled sizes, shapes and magnetisations, metal or metal/oxide nanoparticles and nanoclusters, functionalised vanadium oxide nanoparticles doped with Mn and Fe with controlled superparamagnetism, TiO2 with properties suitable for applications in photodynamic therapy, biocompatible graphene oxide functionalised with polymers conjugated with cytostatic drugs, ZnO and MgO, pure and doped with transition metal ions (Mn, Cu, Fe, Co), functionalised with azole-type compounds, for applications in anti-infective therapies;
  • Synthesis of hydrogels with photo-oxidative or magnetic properties by dispersing TiO2 or magnetite nanoparticles with suitable characteristics in the polymeric structure of the hydrogel;
  • Determination of the behaviour of the nanostructured systems produced under in vitro biological conditions, examining aspects related to cytotoxic/cytostatic action, induction of oxidative stress, and internalisation into cells;
  • Continuous updating of the erris.gov.ro platform by presenting the research services to public- and private-law institutions active in the field of the project;
  • Development of the joint RDI programme correlated with the institutional development plan for the partners involved in the consortium.

Component project 2

Personalised implant systems made of advanced biomaterials with multiple pathways of action

General objective: development of biomaterial demonstrators for a new generation of implants with potential for transfer to economic operators.

Specific objectives:

  • Production of Fe-Pd shape-memory alloys with anti-thrombogenic/thrombolytic surface functionalisation intended for applications in cardiovascular medicine;
  • Generation of ceramic biomaterial demonstrators based on cationically substituted hydroxyapatite for personalised ceramic scaffold prostheses (manufactured by additive manufacturing based on a real bone defect model) and/or biofunctionalisation of dental implants;
  • Testing of the biofunctional response (release of ions with therapeutic potential, cytotoxicity, osteogenesis, angiogenesis, reduction of reactive oxygen species, antimicrobial effect) by performing complex in vitro biological tests;
  • Continuous updating of the erris.gov.ro platform by presenting the research services to public- and private-law institutions active in the field of the project;
  • Development of the joint RDI programme correlated with the institutional development plan for the partners involved in the consortium.

Component projecT 3

New (bio)sensor platforms based on functionalised nanostructured materials with applications in the health field

General objective: development of selective and sensitive (bio)sensors for (i) monitoring the bioavailability of pharmaceutical compounds, (ii) detecting reactive oxygen species, and also for (iii) monitoring their effect on cells and cellular components (e.g. proteins, DNA).

Specific objectives:

  • Manufacture and electrochemical, optical and structural characterisation of single-use electrodes based on carbon, graphene and nanostructured metals;
  • Functionalisation of the electrodes with (bio)materials and optical and structural characterisation;
  • Investigation of the characteristics of the biosensors (reproducibility, stability, interference, etc.);
  • Research services offered to the PCCDI partners;
  • Continuous updating of the erris.gov.ro platform by presenting the research products and services to public- and private-law institutions active in the field of the project;
  • Development of the joint RDI programme correlated with the institutional development plan for the partners involved in the consortium.
