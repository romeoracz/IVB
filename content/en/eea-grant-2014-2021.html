EEA Grant 2014-2021, Contract No 6/2019

Improving the quality of life of patients with autism spectrum disorder by promoting strategies for early diagnosis and preventive measures

http://eea-grant-autism.ro

Consortium

Project Promoter: ”Prof Dr Alexandru Obregia” Clinical Psychiatry Hospital, Bucharest, Romania

Partner 1: University of Oslo, Oslo, Norway

Partner 2: National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, Bucharest, Romania

Abstract

Abstract: The project addresses autism spectrum disorders (ASD), complex neurodevelopmental conditions associated with long-term difficulties and with a significant social impact, in the context of a significant increase in the number of individuals diagnosed with ASD over the last decade. Our project proposes a translational approach, from the clinic to neurobiology and genetics, carried out in partnership between the „Prof. Dr. Alex. Obregia” Clinical Psychiatry Hospital, the National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Biomedical Sciences „Victor Babeș” and the University of Oslo. The comprehensive assessment of a large cohort of patients with ASD will contribute to the initiation of a National ASD Registry. In addition, data will be generated on the genetic mechanisms involved in the development of ASD. The project will enable the teams in Romania to increase their expertise and research capacity. The main objective of the project is to improve the early recognition and clinical diagnosis of ASD, to develop and implement a comprehensive protocol for genetic and neuroimaging studies; to develop patient-centred recommendations; and to initiate a National Registry for ASD.

This project is funded from the EEA Grants 2014 – 2021 Research Programme funds and is led by the Project Promoter, the ”Prof Dr Alexandru Obregia” Clinical Psychiatry Hospital, together with UEFSCDI.
