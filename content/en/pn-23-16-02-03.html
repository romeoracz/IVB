PN 23.16.02.03

Molecular approaches in 3D models (tumour spheroids) gene-edited by the CRISPR/Cas9 method in developing solutions for personalised medicine in cancer

Project funded by the Ministry of Research, Innovation and Digitalisation,

Project code: PN 23.16.02.03

Implementation period: 2023-2026

Contractor: National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”

Project leader: CSII Dr. Codrici Elena

Team: Ana-Maria Enciu, Ionela-Daniela Popescu, Maria Dudau, Lucian Albulescu, Tudor Emanuel Fertig, Aurora Arghir, Sorina Papuc and Cristiana Tanase

Aim: to develop 3D in vitro experimental tumour models (multicellular tumour spheroids), starting from cells gene-edited for genes with an impact on metabolic reprogramming (IDH1, CD36), as an innovative approach in screening for chemotherapy resistance.

Specific scientific objectives:

Objective 1

Generation of 3D in vitro experimental tumour models / multicellular spheroids from standardised cell lines (tumour cells and support cells), with a view to making antitumour therapy more efficient and personalised.

Objective 2

Gene editing of breast cancer (CD36) and glioblastoma (IDH1) tumour cells using CRISPR/Cas9 technology, in order to obtain gene-edited spheroids, targeting the cause of disease / factors influencing health; verification and confirmation of the editing.

Objective 3

Functional evaluation of the gene-edited cells and of the spheroids obtained with these cells versus control (wild type), by proteomic and bioimaging methodologies

Objective 4

Validation of the gene-edited multicellular tumour spheroid models versus control (wild type) in the therapy of the two types of cancer; identification of new mechanisms of resistance to therapy by analysing the differences arising after chemotherapy treatment between gene-edited and non-edited tumour spheroids;

Degree of originality and novelty:

1) enrichment of 3D tumour cultures with support cells

2) CRISPR/Cas9 gene editing

3) generation of gene-edited 3D testing models.
