Laboratory Equipment

4D - Nucleofactor

Nucleofection unit for cuvettes and 16-well plates, with predefined programmes for adherent and suspension cell lines and primary cultures, with the possibility of optimisation and easy data export.
It offers flexibility for various substrates, from plasmids to RNP complexes and peptides.
Applications: gene overexpression or silencing, protein expression, generation of stable clones. The consumables are dedicated.

Laminar flow hood - Holten MaxiSafe 2010

, class IIA, with the following features:

– 2 HEPA filters in the upper part – a main filter and an exhaust filter.

– built-in UV lamp and standard disinfection programme,

– anti-UV protection and electrically activated panel,

– 6 mm thick glass wall, operated through a digital microprocessor panel

– LCD display, laminar flow status indicator, with built-in alarm.

CO2 incubator - ShelLab

with a double casing with an outer water jacket, ideal for cell cultures. The design provides a uniform temperature, which prevents sudden changes and cellular thermal shock, as well as a stable CO2 atmosphere that is easy to maintain and control.

Inverted microscope - Eclipse TS 100 Nikon

with phase contrast and a fluorescence lamp, it is useful for the study of cell cultures and for immunofluorescence experiments. The light source is provided by eco-friendly LEDs, with low consumption and a long operating life of up to 60000 hours.

Luminex 200 platform

The equipment allows multiplex determinations – up to 100/500 analytes in a single sample. The technology can be used to determine protein expression profiles, with extension to genotyping and to establishing gene expression profiles. The system includes the Luminex XY™ reader, the Luminex SD™ fluid delivery unit, software (xPonent 3.1) and a computer.

xMAP (Multi-Analyte Profiling) technology introduces, as an innovative solution, fluorescently labelled microspheres as a binding support for the molecules of interest, using a patented process for incorporating a unique mixture of 2 or 3 fluorochromes. Depending on the degree of incorporation of these fluorochromes, with a reading spectrum in the red and infrared, 100 distinct populations of polystyrene microspheres/500 populations in the case of polystyrene microspheres with magnetic properties can be obtained.

xMAP array multiplexing is an advanced proteomic technology that allows the simultaneous identification and quantification of x-plex biomarker panels from a very small volume of biological sample (serum, plasma, cell supernatant, cell/tissue lysates, urine, cerebrospinal fluid, etc) in a relatively short time, with high accuracy, offering the advantage of an impressive range of applications in scientific research and clinical diagnosis.

Applications:

  • analysis of x-plex sets of pro/anti-inflammatory cytokines to assess the anti-inflammatory and analgesic effects of bioproducts,
  • analysis of signalling molecules in order to assess the modulation of intracellular signalling under the action of bioproducts,
  • investigation of the molecular bases of the biosafety and efficacy of bioproducts,
  • creation of a complex investigation system for implementing proteomic tools to assess the immunotoxicity profile of bioproducts.

Microplate Multimode Detector Zenyth 3100 (Anthos Zenyth platform)

The equipment supports almost all types of applications in molecular biology, biochemistry, environmental and food testing, toxicology and drug screening.

Applications and detection technologies: absorbance and fluorescence reading, polarised light reading, TiRF, FRET, UV/VIS absorbance. It will be used for the quantification of proteins and nucleic acids, protein-protein (receptor-ligand) interaction studies, enzymology assays, reporter assays, determination of phosphorylated proteins, enzymatic activity.

Miniprotean II BioRad vertical electrophoresis and transfer system (Western blot)

When used at full capacity, the system allows the separation of a maximum of 56 samples simultaneously. The gels obtained can be stained and analysed, or can be further subjected to membrane transfer for the identification of certain proteins by immunodetection (western blot). Identification by western blot is completed by reading the result using the c-Digit chemiluminescence detection scanner.

Typhoon FLA 9000 equipment

Laser scanner, useful for biomolecular imaging applications, including high-sensitivity measurements of isotopes, fluorochromes or chemiluminescence signal.

Applications: 2-D DIGE, multiplex fluorescence (in the visible and NIR – near infrared range), chemiluminescent western blot, as well as gel stains (Coomassie™ blue and silver staining).

Features: capture of radioisotopic, fluorescent or chemiluminescent signal, high resolution and quantification, can be optimised by adding detectors, filters and lasers, high-throughput with simultaneous detection of up to 16 blots or 10×8 cm gels, 2-D DIGE imaging.

C-DiGit Blot Scanner, LICOR

Multiscan, multi-exposure equipment that offers the possibility of capturing chemiluminescence signals by linear scanning.

The short distance between the photon source and the detector enables fast and accurate detection of chemiluminescence signals. The linear scanning sensor also contributes to this, efficiently capturing all light signals and making unnecessary the supercooling on which new-generation digital cameras rely.

The device is small and portable, does not require a darkroom or radiographic film, and acquires the image directly from the blotting membrane.

The resolution of the device is 196 µm x 196 µm and it has two acquisition times: standard, 6 minutes, and high, 12 minutes. The image obtained can be adjusted to any intermediate exposure time within this interval. The scanner comes with a dedicated laptop with its own easy-to-use software.
