Cell Biology, Neurosciences and Experimental Myology

a research unit that combines the expertise of a research team with solid experience in cell biology and neurosciences.

At present, our research activity focuses on understanding the cellular and molecular details of physiological and pathological mechanisms across 4 distinct research themes:

  • Neurodegenerative diseases: Characterisation of the effect of bacterial functional amyloid proteins and other amyloidogenic products of the gut microbiota on pathological protein aggregation and on the propagation of pathology according to a prion-like model in models of neurodegeneration, with a view to identifying biomarkers and molecular targets for the development of personalised prophylactic and therapeutic strategies. in sporadic Parkinson’s disease and other neurodegenerative diseases, thus contributing to improving the approach to pathologies with a major impact on health.
  • Skeletal muscle regeneration in normal and dystrophic muscles, with the aim of identifying new and potentially more reliable biomarkers of disease progression for chronic and genetic diseases.
  • Cancer research: epigenetic changes at the level of DNA methylation and post-translational modifications of histones; Natural bioactive compounds acting as epigenetic modulators in chemoprevention – in vitro studies.
