Core Programme 2023-2026

Molecular mechanisms in major non-communicable diseases – from cancer to degenerative pathology, from the genetic to the immune dimension

Acronym: MEMOGEN

Programme director: Prof. Dr. Mihail Eugen Hinescu

Programme objectives:

Ob1. Identification of the molecular mechanisms that determine the pathogenesis of non-communicable diseases, with diagnostic and therapeutic impact

Ob2. Identification and/or validation of molecular targets in cancer for adapting the therapeutic strategy

Ob3. Maintaining and improving the highly specialised human resources of INCD “Victor Babeș”

Ob4. Maintaining and developing the RD infrastructure of INCD “Victor Babeș”

OBJECTIVE 1

Project 1: Advanced strategy for exploring molecular signatures with diagnostic and therapeutic impact in childhood epileptic encephalopathies
Project 2: Sarcopenia associated with neurodegenerative diseases: defining the molecular basis of interrelation on the brain–striated muscle axis to identify prognostic factors and therapeutic targets
Project 3: The role of caveolin-1 in vascular amyloidosis associated with the ageing process
Project 4: The functional triad in experimental murine psoriasis: immune system – skin

OBJECTIVE 2

Project 5: The lncRNA-mediated ceRNA network – a therapeutic target in pancreatic cancer

Project 6: Combinatorial therapeutic approach based on adoptive transfer of NK cells and a TGFBRI/II inhibitor in metastatic melanoma

Project 7: Molecular approaches in 3D models (tumour spheroids) gene-edited by the CRISPR/Cas9 method in developing solutions for personalised medicine in cancer

Project 8: Stress genes as therapeutic targets in radiotherapy and photodynamic therapy of colon carcinoma
