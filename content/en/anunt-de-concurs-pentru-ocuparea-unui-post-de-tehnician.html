Competition notice for filling a technician post

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, with its registered office in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, nr. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition, within the project “Nonspecific oral pain – a multidisciplinary approach for diagnosis and monitoring”, acronym BMS_BIOMARKERS, funded through PN-III-P2-2.1-PED-2019- 1339, Contract 564PED/ 2021, for one technician post in the Radiobiology Laboratory: higher education in the biomedical field (student in the 4th or 5th year at a Faculty of Medicine / Biology / (Bio)Chemistry); half time, fixed term of 2 months. Application files may be submitted daily between 29 August and 27 September 2022, from 13:00 to 15:00, at the Human Resources Office.
