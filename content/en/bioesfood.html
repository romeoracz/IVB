Summary

Recent scientific evidence suggests that fatty acids and volatile oils can act as preventive agents in oxidative stress and inflammatory processes and can modulate epigenetic mechanisms. By bringing them together in an innovative food supplement formulation, they are expected to act synergistically by inducing a multivalent biological activity for disease prevention. BIOESFOOD aims to develop a bioproduct enriched with omega 3 and omega 6 acids and volatile oils extracted from plants grown organically by the Hofigal company. Ecological extraction methods will be applied to preserve the biologically active compounds. The project will start from the pre-existing TRL 2 (technology concept formulated) — for the production and characterisation of biocomponents that show antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity and epigenetic modulator capacity. Biocompatibility studies on hepatic cell lines and monocytes will be carried out throughout the technological processes in order to obtain optimised bioformula variants with enhanced biological activity. The selected bioformulas will enter TRL 3, where in vitro studies will be performed to demonstrate the multivalent biological activity, including the molecular mechanism of action. The project will reach TRL 4 technology, with laboratory validation through a preclinical study on an animal model (mouse) to test its hepatoprotective potential.

General objective of the project:

Development of a food supplement (bioformula) enriched in polyunsaturated fatty acid fractions and essential oils extracted from plants, and validation of its biological activity by testing its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory and epigenetic modulator capacity on cell models. The potential of the bioformula to prevent acute inflammation will be demonstrated on an animal model.

SO1) Obtaining biocomponents extracted from plants and characterising them through physico-chemical analyses, and carrying out biocompatibility studies on cell models;

SO2) Development of an optimised bioformulation of the food supplement based on the evaluation of its biological activities on cell models;

SO3) Demonstration of the preventive potential of the selected bioformula in acute inflammation through a pilot preclinical study on a mouse model.

The activities carried out within the stage were:

  • Selection and characterisation of the plant material
  • Extraction of oils rich in fractions of omega 3, omega 6 and omega 9 polyunsaturated acids from seeds by cold pressing. Their characterisation by physico-chemical methods.

The following were obtained and characterised:

  • milk thistle seed oil (Sylibum marianum)
  • hemp seed oil (Cannabis sativa)
  • flaxseed oil (Linum Usitatissimum)
  • safflower seed oil (Carthamus tinctorius)
  • Extraction of essential/volatile oils by hydrodistillation. Their characterisation by physico-chemical methods.

The following were obtained and characterised:

  • essential oil obtained from peppermint leaves (Mentha piperita L)
  • pine leaf essential oil (Pinus sylvestris)
  • rosemary leaf essential oil ( officinalis)
  • Development of experimental cell models for testing the biocompatibility of fatty and essential oils;

The working protocol was developed for testing the biocompatibility of the extracted oils on in vitro cell models: immortalised human liver epithelial cells (THLE-2) and human hepatocarcinoma epithelial cells (HepG2).

  • Testing of the biocomponents (fatty oils and essential oils) on cell models to establish cytotoxicity, their effect on cell proliferation and the intracellular absorption capacity of the oils
