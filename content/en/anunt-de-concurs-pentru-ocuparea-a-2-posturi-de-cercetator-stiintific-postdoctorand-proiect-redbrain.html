Competition announcement for filling one position of postdoctoral scientific researcher, REDBRAIN project

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, headquartered in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, no. 99-101, sector 5, whose main field of activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition, within the project Knowledge transfer in the field of redox biology for developing advanced molecular tools in neurodegenerative diseases – the signature of the Nrf2 transcription factor for diagnosis and therapy”, acronym REDBRAIN, SMIS Code: P_37_732, coordinated by Prof. Antonio Cuadrado and funded through the Competitiveness Operational Programme, Priority Axis 1: Research, Technological Development and Innovation (RDI) in Support of Economic Competitiveness and Business Development; Action 1.1.4: Attracting staff with advanced competences from abroad to strengthen R&D capacity), for one position of scientific researcher, postdoctoral, speciality biologist, biochemist, pharmacist or another specialisation relevant to biomedical research; full time, fixed term of approximately 2 months (until the end of the project on 31.08.2022). The competition documentation is to be sent between 5 May 2022 – 03 June 2022 by email (gina.manda @ gmail.com, gina.manda[ at] ivb.ro). Further details on the position requirements can be found at www.ivb.ro and www.redbrain.ro.
