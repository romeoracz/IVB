Competition notice for two research positions

Session 5, 2026

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, with its registered office in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței nr. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition for the following research positions in the Laboratory of Ultrastructural Pathology and Bioimaging:

1 post of research assistant in veterinary medicine (COR 225005)

  1. employment contract: fixed-term, 36 months, until 30.11.2029, with the possibility of extension
  2. minimum gross salary for the post: 2,163 RON
  3. part time (half time; 4 hours / day, 20 hours / week, of which 4 hours / day, 20 hours / week within the project Centre of Excellence in Advanced Translational Research in Cardiovascular and Neurovascular Diseases (SEARCH-VASC), contract 10CoEx/2026)

1 post of research assistant in medicine (COR 221110)

  1. employment contract: fixed-term, 36 months, until 30.11.2029, with the possibility of extension
  2. minimum gross salary for the post: 2,163 RON
  3. part time (half time; 4 hours / day, 20 hours / week, of which 4 hours / day, 20 hours / week within the project Centre of Excellence in Advanced Translational Research in Cardiovascular and Neurovascular Diseases (SEARCH-VASC), contract 10CoEx/2026)
  • the application file may be sent electronically to daniela.lungu@ivb.ro;
  • the competition will take place at the headquarters of the National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”;
  • the timetable of the competition tests, the topics and the bibliography are posted in the section dedicated to the competition;
  • the full list of documents in the application file is in accordance with art. 5 of the Framework methodology for competitions to fill vacant research posts, posted in the section dedicated to the competition;
  • the job description, the application form, the declaration of consent to the processing of personal data, the declaration of agreement to the competition results being posted on the institute’s website, the sworn declaration that the information in the application file is true and refers to the candidate’s own activities and achievements, and the INCDVB evaluation form are posted in the section dedicated to the competition;
  • publications that are not available electronically must be submitted as copies.

Application files may be submitted daily between 21.09.2026 and 20.10.2026, from 13:00 to 15:00,
at the Human Resources Office.
