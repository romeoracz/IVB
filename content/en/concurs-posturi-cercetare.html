Competition notice for filling research positions

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, with its registered office in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, nr. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition, within the project “Creation, Operationalisation and Development of the National Competence Centre in the field of cancer – CNCC” under the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR) – PNRR I5 Establishment and operationalisation of Competence Centres, component project CD 5 entitled Standardisation and automation of the process of early and precision diagnosis and predictive testing in colorectal cancer to optimise the time to obtain the final result, for the following posts (fixed term: until 31.12.2025; part time; variable number of hours / month): 3 technician posts and 1 expert post (scientific researcher grade II; speciality medicine / biology / (bio)chemistry or any other relevant to the biomedical field). The competition is organised in accordance with the provisions of art. 13 para. (12) of Law no. 183 of 10 June 2024 on the status of research, development and innovation staff. The competition files will contain a CV (Europass format), copies of the education documents, and any other document the candidate considers relevant to the position applied for. The selection will be held between 16 October 2024 – 29 October 2024.
