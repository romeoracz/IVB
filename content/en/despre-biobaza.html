All activities involving laboratory animals are carried out in accordance with the recognised principles of Directive 2010/63/EU and national legislation. The Biobase Laboratory has dedicated areas that allow the following activities: breeding, maintenance, quarantine, an area for animals under experiment, areas for procedures. The animals are housed in cages with unrestricted access to food and water, under controlled environmental conditions (temperature, humidity, light). The animals’ health is monitored daily by qualified staff and periodically through specific tests.

The following activities are carried out within the Biobase Laboratory:

  • Production of laboratory animals, mice and rats.
  • In vivo testing activity. In vivo testing refers to the use of animals in experimental procedures for research contracts or commercial contracts.

At present, the Biobase Laboratory houses mice (C57BL/6J, BALB/c) and rats (Wistar, Sprague Dawley), as well as genetically modified mice from the Jackson Laboratory (Bar Harbor, ME, USA): B6.129S1-Cd36˂tm1Mfe˃/J, B6.129P2-Apoetm1Unc/J, Kmt2a˂tm2 (MLLT3)Thr˃/KsyJ, CAV1tm1Mls/J, etc.

Working protocols for in vivo experiments may be provided by the beneficiary or may be established together with the researchers of the laboratory.
