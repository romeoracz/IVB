Selection announcement for filling a research position

Session 6 of 2025

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, headquartered in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, no. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces the selection of personnel within the project “Creation, Operationalisation and Development of the National Competence Centre in the field of cancer – CNCC” under the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR) – PNRR I5 Establishment and operationalisation of Competence Centres, component project CD 5 entitled Standardisation and automation of the early and precision diagnosis process and of predictive testing in colorectal cancer in order to optimise the time to obtain the final result as follows:

  1. expert, research assistant in biology, COR 213137
  2. duration of the individual employment contract (CIM): 2 months until 31.12.2025
  3. minimum salary for the position: 2.880 RON / month
  4. part-time work: 40 h / month
  5. the complete list of documents in the file for participation in the selection, in accordance with Art. 7 of the Framework Methodology for competitions for filling positions for the participation of the research organisation's own personnel in research projects, professional training projects and/or other types of projects, is displayed in the section dedicated to session 6 / 2025 on the ivb.ro website;
  6. the job description, the application form for the selection, the declaration of consent to the processing of personal data, and the declaration of agreement to the publication of the selection results on the institute's website are displayed in the section dedicated to session 6 / 2025 on the ivb.ro website.
  7. the selection timetable is displayed in the section dedicated to session 6 / 2025 on the ivb.ro website;

The personnel selection is organised in accordance with the provisions of Art. 13 para. (12) of Law no. 183* of 10 June 2024 on the status of research, development and innovation personnel. The selection files will contain a CV (Europass format), copies of education documents, and any other document that the candidate considers relevant to the position for which they are applying. Files will be submitted between 14 October 2025 and 28 October 2025.
