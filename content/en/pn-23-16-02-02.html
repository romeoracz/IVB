PN 23.16.02.02

Combinatorial therapeutic approach based on adoptive transfer of NK cells and a TGFBRI/II inhibitor in metastatic melanoma

CSII Dr. Gheorghița ISVORANU

Presentation

The project proposes an innovative approach to a therapeutic method based on cytokine-activated NK cells and the administration of a TGF-β receptor inhibitor, which could offer an improved method for the therapy of metastatic melanoma.

The aim of the project is to establish the molecular profile of NK cells for monitoring the antitumour response and to identify an innovative method of immunotherapy in metastatic melanoma, taking into account the current trends of precision medicine in advanced stages of cancer.

Objectives:

  • Generation of cytokine-activated NK cells
  • In vitro validation of the increased antitumour activity of cytokine-activated NK cells and of treatment with a TGFBRI/II inhibitor
  • In vivo validation of the increased antitumour activity of cytokine-activated NK cells and of treatment with a TGFBRI/II inhibitor as a potential candidate for cancer therapy, using murine melanoma models

Expected results:
– Obtaining NK cells with increased cytotoxic activity.
– Identification of possible molecular markers on NK cells for monitoring the antitumour response.
– Identification and evaluation of an optimal therapeutic scheme of adoptive transfer of NK cells in metastatic melanoma.
– Identification and evaluation of optimal doses of TGFBRI/II for preclinical administration as a treatment in metastatic melanoma.
– Validation of a possible therapeutic solution for metastatic melanoma based on adoptive transfer of NK cells and a TGFBRI/II inhibitor.
