Specific objectives

  1. Continuous professional training of human resources;
  2. Adapting the RD of INCD “Victor Babeș” to the objectives of the health system;
  3. Strengthening the Institute's position as a leader in Romanian medical research;
  4. Enhancing national and international visibility;
  5. Good cooperation with partners;
  6. Enhancing performance in the Institute's activity;
  7. Enhancing the efficiency of dissemination of research results;
  8. Reducing the average age of human resources;
  9. Increasing the efficiency and performance of management;
  10. Making more efficient the research services, the transfer of competences and technology, and the services of the Institute's Diagnostic Centre;
  11. Education and professional training actions, attracting young people to RD activity.
