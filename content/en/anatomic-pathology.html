Histopathology and Immunohistochemistry Laboratory

With the top of the art, new equipment and technology and newly renovated, our Anatomic Pathology Department is an excellent environment to perform our research endeavors

The Histopathology and Immunohistochemistry Laboratory is a research and diagnostic unit specialising in transformative research in the field of pathology, such as molecular, digital, computational and diagnostic pathology. We are a group of young and enthusiastic researchers, focused on transforming the field of pathology by investigating and understanding the causes of various pathologies such as cancer and cardiovascular diseases. We are currently developing new imaging and molecular methods for diagnosis and advanced treatment (such as Expansion Pathology/Microscopy (Expansion Pathology/Microscopy), implementing digital pathology and applying advanced ‘machine learning” methods to answer critical questions in the field of pathology and medical sciences. The Histopathology and Immunohistochemistry Laboratory, with state-of-the-art equipment/technology and a space suited to current requirements, provides an ideal environment for development and performance.
