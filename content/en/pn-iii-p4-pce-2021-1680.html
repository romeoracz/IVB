INVESTIGATION OF THE TISSUE EXTRACELLULAR MATRIX BY AUTOMATED 3D TISSUE RECONSTRUCTION

Despite the huge progress of recent decades, the treatment of cardiovascular diseases still has gaps in many respects, and these diseases are still the main cause of death in developed countries. This is partly due to the controversial data resulting from experimental research, which vary depending on groups and protocols, but also to the lack of methods for analysing such complex data resulting from each experiment.

The project offers a solution for standardising and automating the analysis of experimental data in fundamental research on cardiovascular diseases. To our knowledge, our approach brings a new concept and strategy, representing a radical development of traditional optical image processing. By automatically recreating the 3D morphology of organs, integrating all tissue stainings and presenting the interrelation between all ongoing pathological processes, without many human and material resources, we will make a significant contribution to the field of cardiovascular research towards more efficient and more reliable animal experiments in the future. Moreover, by developing an image analysis system based on machine learning, all information on the quantitative investigation of morphological and molecular information from previous experiments will be taken into account. In this way, we align experimental research with the current regulations of the European Union to reduce, refine and replace animal experiments.
