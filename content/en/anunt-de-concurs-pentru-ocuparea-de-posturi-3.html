Selection announcement for filling 3 research posts

Competition announcement

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, headquartered in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, no. 99-101, sector 5, whose main field of activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition, within the project “Creation, Operationalisation and Development of the National Competence Centre in the Field of Cancer – CNCC” under the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR) – PNRR I5 Establishment and operationalisation of Competence Centres, component project CD 5 entitled Standardisation and automation of the early and precision diagnosis process and predictive testing in colorectal cancer to optimise the time to obtain the final result, for 3 posts, as follows:

  1. expert, research assistant in medicine, COR 221110
  2. duration of the individual employment contract (CIM): 7 months until 31.12.2025
  3. minimum salary for the post: 5.750 RON
  4. part-time work: 80 hours / month
  5. the application file in electronic format may be sent to daniela.lungu@ivb.ro
  6. the competition will take place at the headquarters of the National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”;
  7. the schedule of the competition tests, the topics and the bibliography are displayed in the section dedicated to the competition on the website ivb.ro;
  8. the complete list of documents in the application file is in accordance with art. 5 of the Framework Methodology for competitions to fill vacant research posts, displayed in the section dedicated to the competition on the website ivb.ro;
  9. the job description, the application form for the competition, the declaration of consent to the processing of personal data, the declaration of agreement to the publication of the competition results on the institute's website, the declaration on one's own responsibility that the information in the application file corresponds to reality and refers to one's own activities and achievements, and the INCDVB-specific evaluation form are displayed in the section dedicated to the competition on the website ivb.ro;
  10. works that are not available in electronic format will be submitted as copies

Application files may be submitted daily between 15.04.2025 – 12.05.2025, from 13:00 – 15:00, at the Human Resources Office. Further information at www.ivb.ro.
