Competition announcement for research posts

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, headquartered in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, no. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition, within the project “Creation, Operationalisation and Development of the National Competence Centre in the field of cancer – CNCC” under the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR) – PNRR I5 Establishment and operationalisation of Competence Centres, component project CD 5 entitled Standardisation and automation of the early and precision diagnostic process and of predictive testing in colorectal cancer to optimise the time to obtain the final result, for one expert post (scientific researcher grade I; speciality medicine / biology / (bio)chemistry or any other relevant to the biomedical field). The competition is organised in accordance with the provisions of art. 13 para. (12) of Law no. 183 of 10 June 2024 on the status of research, development and innovation personnel. Application files will contain a CV (Europass format), copies of education documents, and any other document the candidate considers relevant to the position applied for.
The selection will take place between 04 November 2024 and 18 November 2024.
