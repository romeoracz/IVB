Description

The development and implementation of a Competence Centre in Cancer entitled: Creation, Operation and Development of the National Competence Centre in the Field of Cancer (NCCC) is focused on improving the management of cancer patients through the development of integrated programmes based on recent advances in the fields of personalised medicine, telemedicine, molecular characterisation of tumours, imaging systems and artificial intelligence. The NCCC is based on a consortium of public and private entities, in accordance with Romania's National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR), Pillar III, section I5. Establishment and operationalisation of Competence Centres PNRR-III-C9-2022 – I5. The development of the NCCC is based on the implementation of 5 CDI projects, whose objectives are interconnected between the private and public partners, with interdisciplinary fields, so that the main aim of this consortium is achieved.

Objectives

The specific objectives of this complex project are:

  1. Creation of a national network of centres (National Cancer Research Network) involved in the management of oncology patients, with routine economic activity (medical activity) and research, development and innovation activity.
  2. Building institutional capacity to improve the collection of quality cancer data in a digitalised network of Regional Cancer Registries, for better management in oncological healthcare and public health strategy.
  3. Develop a digital collaboration platform in support of the NCCC to accelerate the digital transformation of research, innovation and health systems.
  4. Development and implementation of the “Virtual and distributed Tumor Board module
  5. Development of a “Tumor Molecular Board” (TMB) service in support of personalised medicine, by analysing and including genetic and omics data in the selection of the most suitable targeted therapy.
  6. Evaluation of genomic and molecular factors together with the social determinants of health for the correct estimation of individual disease risk.
  7. Development of an integrated approach based on digital pathology and biomarker profiles to improve the accuracy of early diagnosis and of the prediction of metastases in cancer patients.
  8. Development of neuroimaging-based pain biomarkers for personalised pain therapy in cancer patients –
  9. Improving the quality of cancer care through comprehensive evaluation of the medical experience of cancer patients –
  10. Evaluation of the role of positive healthcare experience in improving the quality of life of cancer patients
  11. Improving the quality of life of cancer patients through early palliative care and supportive measures
  12. Dissemination of research results, support activities and implementation of the objectives of the EU cancer missions under Horizon Europe in Romania

General objectives of Project 5

Ob1. Development of an integrated set/panel of biomarkers for the early detection of colorectal cancer,

Ob2. Development of a precise method for predicting metastases in colorectal cancer,

Ob3. Implementation of the digital pathology laboratory and of AI methods to facilitate a better understanding of cancer and to improve precision diagnosis in colorectal cancer.

Specific objectives:

WP 1.

O5.1.1. Development of an integrated panel of biomarkers for the early detection of colorectal cancer.

O5.1.2. Standardisation of the procedures for the collection, preservation and transport of tissue samples for molecular analysis within the inter/regional network.

O5.1.3 Evaluation of the local and systemic inflammatory status and of molecular and angiogenic markers in the early stage of the disease.

WP 2.

O5.2.1. Standardisation of the procedures for the collection, preservation and transport of blood samples for molecular analysis within an inter/regional network.

O5.2.2. Development of new minimally invasive predictive tests for the dissemination of colorectal cancer.

O5.2.3 Evaluation of the local and systemic inflammatory status and of molecular and angiogenic markers in postoperative follow-up.

WP 3.

O5.3.1 Establishment of standard protocols for the collection and processing of tissues for histopathological and molecular diagnosis in colorectal cancer.

O5.3.2. Definition of a workflow for producing digital slides (WSI) with application in medical practice and research.

O5.3.3. Integration of virtual slides (WSI) into the patient's electronic record.

O5.3.4. Development of an algorithm for pre-processing virtual images in colorectal cancer .

O5.3.5. Configuration of a web platform for sharing virtual images and for AI-assisted analysis.
