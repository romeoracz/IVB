HOLIDAY SCHOOL -theoretical module- 20-22 February 2023

Dear participants,

The theoretical module of the Holiday School aims to familiarise you with the research topics implemented through projects at the ”Victor Babeș” National Institute of Pathology and to present to you the scientific methods of fundamental research used by researchers to demonstrate their hypotheses. After completing this module, you will be able to experience these methods yourselves, hands-on, in the next, practical module, which will take place starting from the 2nd week of July, at our Institute.

To be able to participate in the practical module, the following conditions must be met cumulatively:

  • Daily attendance at the presentations of the theoretical module;
  • Submission of a research project plan, as a team – 3 participants/team, following the model that will be presented to you during the theoretical module.

The members of the teams with the best 5 projects will be invited in the summer to the institute's laboratories, to explore a new laboratory each day, alongside our researchers, as part of the practical module of the Holiday School.
