Competition announcement for filling one research assistant position

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, headquartered in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, no. 99-101, sector 5, whose main field of activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition, within the Immunology Laboratory, for one research assistant position (PhD student; speciality medicine/biology/(bio)chemistry or any other speciality relevant to the biomedical field; full time; fixed term of 36 months). Competition files can be submitted daily between 01 September – 30 September 2023 , between 13:00 – 15:00, at the Human Resources Office.
