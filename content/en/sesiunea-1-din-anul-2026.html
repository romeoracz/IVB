Competition for filling research support positions (entry-level technician)

Session 1 of 2026

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, headquartered in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, no. 99-101, sector 5, whose main field of activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition, in the Ultrastructural Pathology and Bioimaging Laboratory, for 2 entry-level technician positions (support staff for RDI activities), as follows:

  1. duration of the individual employment contract (CIM): indefinite
  2. minimum gross salary for the position: 4.050 RON
  3. full time (8 hours / day, 40 hours / week)
  4. the application file in electronic format can be sent to daniela.lungu@ivb.ro
  5. the competition will take place at the headquarters of the National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”;
  6. the schedule of the competition tests, the topics and the bibliography are posted in the section dedicated to the competition on the website ivb.ro;
  7. the complete list of documents in the competition file is in accordance with art 5 of the Framework methodology for competitions for filling vacant research positions posted in the section dedicated to the competition on the website ivb.ro;
  8. the job description, the application form for the competition, the declaration of consent for the processing of personal data, the declaration of agreement to the publication of the competition results on the institute's website, the declaration on one's own responsibility that the information in the competition file is true and refers to one's own activities and achievements, and the INCDVB-specific evaluation form are posted in the section dedicated to the competition on the website ivb.ro;
  9. works that are not available in electronic format will be submitted as copies
  10. Additional information:
    1. level of education: secondary or higher.
    2. no previous experience is required
    3. knowledge of English is an advantage
