Competition notice for filling technician positions

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, with its registered office in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, nr. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition for three technician positions (fraction of full time: ¼ – 1/2; fixed term), within an exploratory research project (contract 153 / 2021) in the Anatomic Pathology Laboratory. Application files may be submitted daily between 11 October – 9 November 2021 from 13:00 – 15:00 at the Human Resources Office.
