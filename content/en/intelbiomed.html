Implementation of biomedical research expertise through knowledge transfer to the private sector for the validation of products and services in the fields of medical biotechnologies and health INTELBIOMED www.intelbiomed.ro

The general objective of the project is the transfer of knowledge/expertise and technology from INCD “Victor Babeș” to private enterprises in the sector of production and development of bioproducts intended for health care, with a view to increasing their economic and scientific competitiveness at national and international level. The project will thus lead to an increase in the socio-economic exploitation of research results in a field that is showing an upward trend internationally, placing INCD “Victor Babeș” in a key position in the bioproducts economy.

Strategic objectives

  • Exploitation of the RDI potential/results of IVB in correlation with the RDI needs of enterprises in the field of medical and pharmaceutical biotechnologies (at least 9 enterprises)
  • Development of a functional framework for long-term collaboration between IVB and the private sector in the fields of medical biotechnologies and health.
  • Direct application of the technologies and methods developed by IVB to ensure rapid and efficient technology transfer to industry and their integration into new/optimised products with high competitive potential.
  • Exploitation of the areas of expertise of the research teams through their direct involvement in the development/improvement of technologies and products in the field of biomedical and pharmaceutical biotechnologies identified by enterprises as being in demand on the market.
  • Promotion of the modern advanced research infrastructure in cellular and molecular medicine of IVB: the biochemistry/proteomics, genomics, immunology and cellular and molecular bio-imaging platform, cell culture facilities, real-time monitoring, biobase, etc.
  • Ensuring access of private enterprises (at least 10) to IVB's specialist expertise and facilities, in order to increase the industrial competitiveness, through innovation, of the enterprises involved in the project.
These objectives place the project within the major field of application of the National Strategy for Competitiveness, as well as other sectoral and regional innovation strategies, through the development of new biotechnological methods, means and products that are efficient both in diagnosis and in therapy for medicine

The project targets

  • The development of medical biotechnologies based on genomics, proteomics, epigenetics, immunotoxicology and imaging: regenerative therapies, biotherapeutics, nanobiotechnologies, etc.;
  • investigation of the molecular bases of the assessment of therapeutic efficacy and the associated biochemical signatures;
  • Minimally invasive techniques and quantitative methods for visualisation, imaging, monitoring and assessment of therapeutic intervention, based on natural bioproducts
  • Development of biopharmaceuticals by targeting specific biochemical processes, exploring new peptides, molecular structures and delivery systems at cellular and intracellular level.
  • Preclinical testing and preparation for clinical testing of products, systems and methods based on biotechnologies, as stages of the pharmaceutical development chain.
