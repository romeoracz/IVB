Selection notice for filling research positions

Session 7, 2025

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, with its registered office in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, nr. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition, within the project “Development of translational research for vaccines, sera and other biological medicines (CANTAVAC 2.0)” of the Health Programme, for 2 posts, as follows:

  1. research assistant (specialisation: biologist, physician, any other specialisation relevant to electron microscopy techniques), COR 221110
  2. duration of the individual employment contract: 48 months until 19.12.2029
  3. minimum salary for the post: 4.840 RON
  4. part-time work: 40 hours / month
  5. the application file in electronic format may be sent to daniela.lungu@ivb.ro
  6. the competition will take place at the headquarters of the National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”;
  7. the schedule of the competition tests, the topics and the bibliography are displayed in the section dedicated to the competition on the ivb.ro website;
  8. the complete list of documents in the application file is in accordance with art 5 of the Framework methodology for competitions to fill vacant research posts, displayed in the section dedicated to the competition on the ivb.ro website;
  9. the job description, the competition application form, the declaration of consent for the processing of personal data, the declaration of agreement to the publication of the competition results on the institute’s website, the statement on own responsibility that the information in the application file is true and refers to the candidate’s own activities and achievements, and the INCDVB-specific evaluation sheet are displayed in the section dedicated to the competition on the ivb.ro website;
  10. works that are not available in electronic format shall be submitted as copies

Application files may be submitted daily between 31.10.2025 and 03.12.2025, from 13:00 to 15:00, at the Human Resources Office.
