Competition notice for filling a research assistant post

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, with its registered office in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, nr. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition for one research assistant post, PhD student, specialising in medicine/biology/(bio)chemistry or any other specialisation relevant to the biomedical field (full time, fixed term of 1 year with the possibility of extension). Application files may be submitted daily between 17 May 2023 and 15 June 2023, from 13:00 to 15:00, at the Human Resources Office. Further information at www.ivb.ro
