NOTICE
of the competition for the post of Director General of the National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeş”

The National Research Authority is organising a competition for the post of director general of the National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeş”, in accordance with Government Decision no. 743 of 18 September 2026 approving the Competition Methodology for the post of director general at national research and development institutes, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 804 of 22 September 2026.

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeş” has its registered office in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenţei nr. 99-101, Sector 5.

Its field/object of activity is fundamental and applied research in human pathology, medical genetics and immunology, and cell and molecular biology, in accordance with Government Decision no. 984/1999 establishing the National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences Victor Babeş, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 588 of 2 December 1999, as subsequently amended and supplemented.

Candidates must meet all the conditions for taking part in the competition set out in art. 172 para. (1) of Law no. 183/2024 on the status of research, development and innovation staff, as subsequently amended and supplemented.

The documents required to enter the competition are those listed in art. 9 of the annex to Government Decision no. 743/2026 approving the Competition Methodology for the post of director general at national research and development institutes.

Candidates register and submit their competition documents on the online platform at: https://www.research.gov.ro/sistemul-de-cercetare/institute-nationale-de-cercetare-dezvoltare/formular-de-inregistrare-candidati-pentru-ocuparea-postului-de-director-general/.
