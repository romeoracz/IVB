Competition notice for filling research posts

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, with its registered office in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, nr. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition for the following posts: 1 position of scientific researcher with doctoral studies, specialising in medicine/biology/(bio)chemistry or any other specialisation relevant to the biomedical field, for assistance with tissue processing, immunohistological imaging and morphometric analysis (half time; fixed term of 12 months); 2 positions of research assistant (master’s student), specialising in medicine/biology/(bio)chemistry or any other specialisation relevant to the biomedical field, for assistance with tissue processing and immunohistology, imaging and analyses, simulation (half time; fixed term of 12 months); 1 position of research assistant (master’s student) for assistance with computer simulation and software development (half time; fixed term of 12 months). Application files may be submitted daily between 11 August and 09 September 2023, from 13:00 to 15:00
at the Human Resources Office.
