Competition announcement for filling one research assistant position

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, headquartered in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, no. 99-101, sector 5, whose main field of activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition for one research assistant position (speciality medicine / pharmacy, (bio)chemistry, biology, any other speciality relevant to the biomedical field). Competition files can be submitted daily between 22 July 2024 – 20 August 2024, between 13:00 – 15:00, at the Human Resources Office.
