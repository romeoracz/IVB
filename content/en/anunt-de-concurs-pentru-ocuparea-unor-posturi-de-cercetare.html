Competition announcement for research positions

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, headquartered in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, no. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition, within the project: PN-III-P1-1.2-PCCDI-2017-0782, “Advanced innovative approaches for predictive regenerative medicine”, acronym – REGMED, for two research assistant positions (speciality biologist, biochemist, chemist or another specialisation relevant to biomedical research; full time, fixed term of 13 months with the possibility of extension) within the Immunobiology Laboratory. Application files can be submitted daily between 29 October 2021 – 27 November 2021, from 13:00 – 15:00, at the Human Resources Office.
