Development of an innovative product based on increasing the therapeutic potential of extracts obtained within CROMATEC PLUS SRL

SMIS 2014+ code: 156316

Call: POC/1033/1/3/Stimulating enterprise demand for innovation through RDI projects carried out by enterprises individually or in partnership with RD institutes and universities, for the purpose of process and product innovation in economic sectors with growth potential

– Innovative technological project –

General objective

The general objective of the project is to diversify the company's activity by developing an innovative product based on increasing the therapeutic potential of extracts obtained within CROMATEC PLUS SRL.

The project aims to develop new formulations by improving and/or developing new types of biovectors (biomimetic complexes) based on medicinal plant extract and biocompatible supports, for the delivery of molecules with therapeutic potential (MPT). The MPT are obtained from a phytocomplex (concentrated extracts from fruits and/or leaves) of Hippophae rhamnoides (sea buckthorn, fam. Elaeagnaceae). In this way, the project follows the direction of diversifying the activity of CROMATEC PLUS through products and technologies that have not previously been produced in the company. The project aims at in vitro testing of the efficiency of the biovector – MPT complexes, and at establishing in vitro the kinetics of controlled release of the active principles – from the biovector-MPT complexes). The possible mechanisms involved in the release of the active principles from the complex matrices will be established.
