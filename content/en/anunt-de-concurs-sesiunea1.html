Selection notice for filling research positions Session 1, 2025

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, with its registered office in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, nr. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a staff selection within the project “Creation, Operationalisation and Development of the National Competence Centre in the field of cancer – CNCC” under the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR) – PNRR I5 Establishment and operationalisation of Competence Centres, component project CD 5 entitled Standardisation and automation of the process of early and precision diagnosis and predictive testing in colorectal cancer to optimise the time to obtain the final result for the following posts:

  1. one expert post, research assistant, doctoral student: (fixed term: until 31.12.2025; part time; 75 hours / month; speciality medicine / biology / (bio)chemistry or any other relevant to the biomedical field);
  2. 1 technician post (fixed term: until 31.12.2025; part time; 14 hours / month)

The staff selection is organised in accordance with the provisions of art. 13 para. (12) of Law no. 183* of 10 June 2024 on the status of research, development and innovation staff. The selection files will contain a CV (Europass format), copies of the education documents, and any other document the candidate considers relevant to the position applied for. Files are to be submitted between 05 March 2025 – 19 March 2025. Further information at www.ivb.ro

*art 13 para. (12) Employment for participation in research projects, projects in the field of education and vocational training or in any other types of projects, regardless of the source of funding, may be for a fixed term, at most equal to the duration of the respective project, without completing other prior formalities and/or selection processes, for the staff named in the staff lists as members of the project team, as well as for the staff of the research organisation. In the case of staff of the research organisation, employment within a project is made on the basis of the decision of the head of the respective organisation, by concluding an individual employment contract and, in the case of higher education institutions, employment is made without supplementing the employee’s basic workload. The salary is granted within the limit of the available funds, according to the provisions of the funding contract/the funder’s rules.
