Competition announcement for one research assistant position, PhD student

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, headquartered in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, no. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition for one position of applications officer, PhD student (equivalent to a research assistant position, speciality medicine/biology/(bio)chemistry or any other speciality relevant to the biomedical field; part-time 40 hours/month, fixed term of approx. 4 months), position 1, within the project “Implementation of biomedical research expertise through knowledge transfer to the private sector for the validation of products and services in the fields of medical biotechnologies and health”, acronym INTELBIOMED, SMIS Code: P_40_197, under the Competitiveness Operational Programme 2014-2020, Priority Axis 1: Research, Technological Development and Innovation (RDI) in Support of Economic Competitiveness and Business Development; Action 1.2.3: Partnerships for knowledge transfer. The responsibilities within the project involve initiating and maintaining cell cultures and toxicoproteomic assays. Application files can be submitted daily between 21 March – 19 April 2022, from 13:00 – 15:00, at the Human Resources Office.
