QX200 Droplet Digital PCR System (Bio Rad)

The instrument allows the quantification of DNA (copy number) in the absence of a standard curve. DNA quantification is based on fluorescent PCR, with SYBRgreen or TaqMan. The system is very useful for: (1) detecting rare mutations; (2) measuring copy number when the differences in target expression level between samples are small (in general, such subtle differences could not be determined by standard qPCR). The main feature of the system is that the PCR mixture is partitioned into approximately 20000 droplets, and each droplet is the equivalent of one PCR reaction.
