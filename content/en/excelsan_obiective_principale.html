Main objectives

  1. Increasing the quality of human resources and individual performance;
  2. Increasing the visibility of INCD “Victor Babeș” at national and international level;
  3. Increasing efficiency in the dissemination and exploitation of research results;
  4. Increasing the capacity to attract research funding in international competitions;
  5. Enhancing the efficiency and capacity of the Institute in providing services (research and diagnostic) and in the transfer of competences and technologies.
