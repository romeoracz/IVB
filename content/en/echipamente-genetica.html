Equipment

DNA MICROARRAY

Equipment set comprising the Agilent DNA SureScan Microarray scanner (purchased through the POSCCE 2.2.1 633/2014 project), Agilent Bioanalyzer, support equipment (hybridisation oven, vortex, mini-centrifuges for chips), computers and specialised software for performing experiments, data extraction and analysis).

The high-resolution scanning equipment for microarray slides, with high detection sensitivity and a dynamic autofocus system, allows the scanning of microarray slides with a high density of oligonucleotide probes

Applications: microarray-based comparative genomic hybridisation – array-CGH – for the analysis of genomic imbalances, and CGH+SNP for the simultaneous identification of DNA copy number variations and regions of loss of heterozygosity.

SEQUENCING

The Sanger sequencing equipment located in the Medical Genetics Laboratory, the Applied Biosystems 3500 Genetic Analyzer (purchased through the POSCCE 2.2.1 633/2014 project), is an integrated platform that combines high-performance hardware, the 8-capillary analyser, and dedicated software for direct sequencing and fragment analysis.

In addition, our team also uses the next-generation targeted sequencing platform – Ion PGM System (ThermoFisher Scientific) located in the Molecular Pathology Laboratory, Anatomic Pathology Department.

Applications: targeted DNA sequencing, fragment analysis by capillary electrophoresis, MLPA

MOLECULAR GENETICS

PCR equipment (Corbett Real Time PCR – Rotor Gene6000, Corbett Palm Cycler) and general molecular genetics equipment.

Applications: PCR-based technologies

OPTICAL MICROSCOPY FOR CYTOGENETIC APPLICATIONS

Motorised Zeiss Axio Z1 microscope with direct transmitted light and epifluorescence examination, equipped with a high-resolution monochrome CCD device; Nikon E800 microscope with direct transmitted light and epifluorescence examination, equipped with a high-resolution monochrome CCD device.

Metasystems software package for automated scanning, detection and image capture for cytogenetics (Metafer and Ikaros software), karyotyping (Ikaros software) and fluorescence in situ hybridisation (M-FISH, m-BAND, Q- FISH and CGH software).

Applications: classical and molecular cytogenetic tests
