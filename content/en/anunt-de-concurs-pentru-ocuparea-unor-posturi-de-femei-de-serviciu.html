Competition notice for filling cleaning staff posts

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, with its registered office in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, nr. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition, within the Administrative Department, for two cleaning staff posts (full time, permanent). Application files may be submitted daily between 12 August 2024 and 09 September, from 13:00 to 15:00, at the Human Resources Office.
