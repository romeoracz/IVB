Session 4, 2026

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, with its registered office in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței nr. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition for the following research positions:

2. 1 post of scientific researcher in biology (COR 213136) in the Immunology Laboratory

  1. employment contract: permanent
  2. minimum gross salary for the post: 4,500 RON

  1. employment contract: permanent
