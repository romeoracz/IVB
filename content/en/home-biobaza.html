has as its main activity the breeding, use and supply of laboratory animals used for scientific purposes.

The Biobase Laboratory of INCD Victor Babeș has as its main activity the breeding, use and supply of laboratory animals used for scientific purposes (Sanitary-veterinary authorisation no. 21/21.11.2024).

All activities involving laboratory animals are carried out in accordance with the recognised principles of Directive 2010/63/EU and national legislation. The Biobase Laboratory has special areas that allow the following activities: breeding, maintenance, quarantine, an area for animals under experiment, and areas for procedures.

The following activities are carried out within the Biobase Laboratory:

  • Production of laboratory animals, mice and rats.
  • In vivo testing activity. In vivo testing refers to the use of animals in experimental procedures for research contracts or commercial contracts.

The Biobase Laboratory currently houses mice (C57BL/6J, BALB/c) and rats (Wistar), as well as genetically modified mice from the Jackson Laboratory (Bar Harbor, ME, USA).

Working protocols for in vivo experiments can be provided by the beneficiary or can be established together with the laboratory's researchers.

Our unit has equipment for metabolic studies, haematological analyses, behavioural tests for assessing the effect of drugs on motor coordination or for assessing learning and memory in animal models of central nervous system disorders, a complete gait analysis system for the quantitative assessment of steps and locomotion (CatWalkTM XT), as well as a system for general and cardiac ultrasound with applications in cardiovascular and inflammatory diseases and cancer (VEVO 210 Ultrasound System).
