Dear colleagues and friends,

This year, too, we invite you to the 2022 International Pathology Conference of the “Victor Babeș” Institute (International Pathology Conference of „Victor Babeș” Institute Bucharest), which will take place on 17 – 19 November 2022, again in virtual format.

Registration for and participation in the Conference proceedings will take place on the conference website: https://ipc2022.event-online.ro/

The registration fee is 150 lei (speakers and students are exempt).

The number of EMC-CMR credits will be announced at a later date.

The following sessions are open for the presentation of your papers:

– Short communication – Young Pathologists

– Short communication – Biomedical research

– Short communication – Young researcher

– Varia/Posters.

Only original papers are accepted. The abstract must be written in English only and must include: the title, authors, affiliation, objectives, methods, results and conclusions. Maximum number of words 300.

Abstracts must be sent in Word format to the address ipc22@ivb.ro, by 1 November 2022.

Entry of a paper for the “Short communication – young researcher” session must be stated explicitly. These papers will be judged and the best original presentation will receive an award.

We have attached the preliminary programme of the conference and we hope you will find topics of interest to you.

Further information at the e-mail address: ipc22@ivb.ro.

We look forward to seeing you,

Mihaela Gherghiceanu, on behalf of the organisers
