Specific objectives:

  1. Continuous adaptation of the Institute's RD to the particular objectives of the national health system, in the context of national and international priorities in the field of biomedical research.
  2. Increasing the visibility of INCD “Victor Babeș” at national level and strengthening its leading position in Romanian medical research, by drawing up priority agendas, identifying new resources, attracting new researchers and/or new research groups into efficient partnerships, and initiating new research networks at both national and international level.
  3. Increasing international visibility and interactions with research groups in Europe and worldwide for the development of relevant, high-impact research.
  4. Continuing the permanent updating of the scientific concerns specific to biomedical research in order to respond to the social and health needs of the population, and good cooperation with existing or newly identified partners from the public or private sector.
  5. Increasing the performance of the Institute's activity in the directions imposed by (i) the needs of the health system (diagnosis, prognosis, therapeutic targets, predominantly tumour pathology, in the spirit of personalised medicine, and neurodegenerative pathologies in accordance with the Joint Programme of Neurodegenerative Diseases (JPND) of the Horizon 2020 programme), (ii) the bioeconomy (knowledge/expertise transfer and technology transfer to private enterprises in the sector of production and development of bioproducts, with a view to increasing their scientific and economic competitiveness at national and international level) and (iii) eco-nanotechnologies (the use of nanoparticles to increase the efficacy of anti-tumour therapy and limit adverse effects).
  6. Reducing the average age of the human resources involved in RD, by promoting the activity among students and hiring young graduates, while maintaining the critical mass of researchers needed for the institutional performance pursued through the development strategy.
  7. Increasing managerial performance at the level of the Institute by improving the management of the research laboratories, achieved through computerisation.
  8. Applying the knowledge resulting from RD activity in diagnostic activity and broadening the range of health services offered by the Institute's Diagnostic Centre, as well as increasing the capacity to translate research results into health policies and practices.
  9. Concern for education and professional training, for attracting young people to RD activity, as well as for the transfer of expertise to the clinic.
