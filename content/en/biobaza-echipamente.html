Equipment

IVC (Individually Ventilated Cages) housing system for mice

The cages are fitted with a HEPA filter that allows air to enter each cage at the same pressure, speed and temperature.

Haematology analyser (Hemavet 950 FS, Drew Scientific Group)

Automated analyser used to determine haematological parameters in laboratory animals (mice, rats, guinea pigs, rabbits).

The analyser determines 20 haematological parameters: WBC, NE#, NE%, LY#, LY%, MO#, MO%, EO#, EO%, BA#, BA%
RBC, Hb, HCT, MCV, MCH, MCHC, RDW, MPV, PLT.

Metabolic cages for mice and rats

Metabolic cages make it possible to measure food and water intake, as well as to separate and collect urine and faeces used for numerous qualitative and quantitative determinations.

Ultrasound scanner for laboratory animals VEVO 210 Ultrasound System

Ultrasonography is an imaging technique that provides anatomical and physiological information in real time. The VEVO 2100 system is easy to use, non-invasive and fast. It is used for cardiac and general ultrasonography in mice and rats.

Tail-Flick

The tail-flick test is a nociception test used in rodents. The test involves applying a heat stimulus to the animal’s tail (mouse or rat) and recording the time the animal needs to withdraw its tail.

Plethysmometer

Device for measuring paw volume and its changes (swelling) in rodents. A useful instrument for assessing the inflammatory response in experimental models and for evaluating the potential anti-inflammatory or anti-oedematous properties of the tested compounds.

Hot/Cold Plate

The Hot/Cold plate device is used to test the thermal sensitivity of animals to pain resulting from exposure to heat or cold, and is a useful instrument for screening analgesic drugs in experimental animal models (rodents).

ROTAROD APPARATUS

The device is used to assess the effect of drugs on motor coordination or resistance to fatigue in rodents. The “Rota-Road” technique is essential in screening drugs that are potentially active or that have side effects on motor coordination.

Apparatus for testing passive avoidance behaviour

The device is used to assess learning and memory in animal models of central nervous system disorders. The passive avoidance test is used to assess memory based on the association formed between a particular context, which the animal learns to avoid, and an aversive stimulus, represented by a mild shock to the limb. The apparatus is divided into two compartments: one is dimly lit and preferred by the animals, and the other is brightly lit.

CatWalk™XT System

The CatWalk™XT system is a highly sensitive instrument for assessing gait and locomotion in laboratory animals, mainly rodents. The animal crosses a glass plate and the footprints are captured by a video camera. The animals move freely, allowing an accurate recording of physiological gait. The system has software that visualises the footprints and statistically calculates footprint dimensions, movement time and the distances between steps. The method is validated in numerous pathological models: Parkinson’s disease, spinal cord injury, neuropathic pain, cerebellar ataxia and other neurological diseases.

COMPACT RADIAL MAZE

The radial maze is a common test of spatial memory in rodents. Both spatial memory (place learning) and non-spatial memory (associative learning) can be assessed in the radial maze. This system is based on the natural tendency of rodents to explore, learn and remember different spatial locations.
