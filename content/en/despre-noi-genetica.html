ABOUT

Our laboratory collaborates continuously with clinics and university departments in Bucharest. By using unique biological material from patients to detect and characterise clinically important genetic changes, the data obtained contribute to enriching knowledge about the pathologies addressed, with the ultimate aim of optimising the clinical management of patients.

The technologies routinely used in research activities are microarray-based comparative genomic hybridisation, targeted next-generation sequencing, PCR technologies and Sanger sequencing, supported by classical and molecular cytogenetic techniques.
