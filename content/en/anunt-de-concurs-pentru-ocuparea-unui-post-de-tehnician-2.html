Competition announcement for a technician post

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, headquartered in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, no. 99-101, sector 5, whose main activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition for one technician post within the Laboratory of Ultrastructural Pathology and Bioimaging: secondary education, full-time, permanent contract. Application files may be submitted daily between 26 June and 25 July 2023, from 13:00 to 15:00, at the Human Resources Office.

All submitted files are administratively eligible
