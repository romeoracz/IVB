Competition announcement for filling two research assistant posts, PhD student/postdoctoral researcher

The National Institute of Research and Development in the Field of Pathology and Biomedical Sciences “Victor Babeș”, headquartered in Bucharest, Splaiul Independenței, no. 99-101, sector 5, whose main field of activity is research and development in the biomedical field, announces a competition for two posts of applications officer, PhD student/postdoctoral researcher (equivalent to research assistant, CS, CS II posts, specialisation medicine/biology/(bio)chemistry/pharmacy; part-time 12 hours/month, fixed term of approx. 11 months), positions 1 and 3, within the project “Implementation of biomedical research expertise through knowledge transfer to the private sector for the validation of products and services in the fields of medical biotechnologies and health”, acronym INTELBIOMED, SMIS Code: P_40_197, under the Competitiveness Operational Programme 2014-2020, Priority Axis 1: Research, Technological Development and Innovation (RDI) in Support of Economic Competitiveness and Business Development; Action 1.2.3: Partnerships for knowledge transfer. The responsibilities within the project involve initiating and maintaining cell cultures and toxicoproteomic assays. Application files may be submitted daily between 24 August – 22 September 2021 from 13:00 – 15:00 at the Human Resources Office.
