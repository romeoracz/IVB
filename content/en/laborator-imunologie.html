Immunology Laboratory

The Immunology Laboratory (IL) is a research unit of the Victor Babeș National Institute of Pathology in Bucharest, Romania, specialised in immunological research

The main research theme of the Immunology Laboratory is the involvement of immune mechanisms in physiological and pathological processes (mainly autoimmune diseases and oncology). The Immunology Laboratory is currently developing projects focused on the evaluation of immune parameters to improve diagnosis, monitor clinical evolution and personalise therapy in skin diseases, mainly autoimmune dysregulation in psoriasis and skin cancers, both melanoma and non-melanoma. Animal models of psoriasis, cutaneous carcinogenesis and cutaneous melanoma have been developed to study the involvement of the immune system in the initiation and evolution of the pathologies studied. New therapies and new approaches in skin pathologies have been developed using the data collected. International collaborations have been established in this field, including the Department of Dermatology, University of Modena, and patents have been completed on both therapeutic approaches and molecular mechanisms in skin pathologies.

In the area of identifying new therapies, the Immunology Laboratory is also developing in the field of immunotoxicology, in an attempt to test the therapies and compounds used in various skin pathologies. In this field, international collaborations have been established with the University of Crete, the University of Santiago de Compostela, the Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia and the University of Salerno. The last and most recently addressed field is the investigation of immune memory in SARSCoV-2 virus infection. In response to the current pandemic, the Immunology Laboratory has developed on-site national collaborations with the hospitals engaged on the front line of the Covid-19 pandemic and has developed international collaborations with the University of Pittsburgh, the University of Verona and the University of Crete.
