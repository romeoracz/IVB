COST CA 17103

Delivery of Antisense RNA Therapeutics

Antisense oligonucleotides (ASOs) are a new class of drugs which, through highly specific targeting, could correct genetic defects in rare inherited diseases, can modulate autoimmune or neurodegenerative diseases, or can target tumours/viruses. However, only a few such drugs are currently on the market, and those that are have a lower efficiency than expected. The main obstacle to their efficacy appears to be their poor delivery to target tissues. While translational research on ASOs is growing, very little is known about the mechanisms by which ASOs are taken up by different specific tissues and cells.

The main aim of the COST Action Delivery of Antisense RNA Therapeutics (DARTER) is to use an interdisciplinary research network bringing together researchers and innovators to investigate the delivery of nucleic acid therapies, with the aim of enabling RNA-targeting nucleic acid drugs to reach their full potential and become a mainstream therapeutic option.

DARTER will act through 3 working groups with research objectives (delivery strategies, model systems, safety and toxicology) and one capacity-building group (stakeholder communication), with the objective of reaching consensus on protocols for evaluating ASO delivery and toxicology, while also pursuing the training of new researchers in the field.

The DARTER COST network brings together participants from COST countries and several international partners, including academics, industrial partners, patient representatives and clinicians, and is open to other stakeholders.
